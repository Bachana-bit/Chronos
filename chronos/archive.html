1923 to today

A century,unwound.

Keep scrolling and the archive moves sideways, one era at a time.

1923

The cabinet

A clockmaker in Milan opens a cabinet of curiosities, filled with broken watches and one working pendulum.

1951

First light

The cabinet becomes a museum. Its first installation is a single candle, timed to burn for exactly one afternoon.

1974

The silver room

Artists begin working in metal and mirrors. The silver room opens and visitors lose all sense of the hour.

1989

Electric hours

Video and electricity arrive. The museum stays open all night for the first time.

2003

The code decade

The first generative works are shown. Nobody can say who finished them, the artist or the machine.

2015

Slow

A decade-long exhibition begins. Each year one object is added and one is quietly taken away.

2026

Everything, now

Chronos opens its doors to the web, and the museum is finally as large as time.
