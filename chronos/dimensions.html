Beyondthree.

An immersive passage through four dimensions. Move your cursor to carry a light through the space, and change the lighting below.

I

Length

The first dimension is a line. A path walked from morning to evening, a thread unspooled behind you. Everything that has ever happened to you sits somewhere along it.

II

Depth

Step sideways and the line becomes a room. Memory is not behind us but beneath us, layered like silt, and every layer still hums.

III

Motion

Add movement and time begins to curve. Objects drift, orbit and fall toward one another, never quite touching, never quite apart.

IV

Memory

The last dimension is the one you carry. It cannot be measured, only felt, and it is the reason a single second can last a lifetime.
